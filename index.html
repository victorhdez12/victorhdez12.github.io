CASE STUDY · INTERNAL PROJECT · OHB

MBSE + AI: engineering answers you can trace back to the model

Non-confidential view. Programme names, data and tooling specifics are omitted.

Problem
Engineering knowledge is split across system models, requirement databases and documents. Answering “what is affected if this changes?” takes hours of manual searching and depends on who you ask.
Approach
Link models, requirements and documentation in a knowledge graph, then let an AI assistant query that graph so every answer points to the elements and documents it came from.
Guardrails
Sources shown with every answer, an engineer validates before anything reaches a deliverable, and project data is handled within its contractual and export-control constraints.
My role
Project lead: use-case definition with engineering teams, scope and roadmap, stakeholder alignment and the link to the company AI roadmap.

AI for proposals: reuse, compliance and drafting

Non-confidential view. Customer and bid details are omitted.

Outcome

FEATURED PUBLICATION · FIRST AUTHOR

Modeling and control techniques for landing on (99942) Apophis: An analysis of a mission scenario

Acta Astronautica, Volume 235, 2025, pp. 185–194.

First-author research carried out at the German Aerospace Center (DLR), analysing the APOSSUM probe’s approach, controlled landing and return in a fast sample-return scenario built around Apophis’ 2029 close encounter with Earth.

The study models Apophis’ gravitational field using spherical harmonics and tetrahedral density distributions, compares PD and Bang-Bang control strategies, and tests robustness under uncertainty.
